Privacy policy

Last updated November 21, 2026. Covers the Plushbucket website (plushbucket.com) and the Plushbucket apps for iPhone, iPad and Android.

The short version

Plushbucket has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. What you enter stays on your device. We don't receive it, sell it or share it.

What stays on your device

Your bikes, forks and shocks, your weight, slider settings, service dates, ride hours and trail notes are saved only on your phone, tablet or computer (in the app, or in your browser's storage on the website). They're used only to calculate your settings and service reminders. Deleting the app or clearing your browser data erases them.

Apple Watch

If you install Plushbucket on your Apple Watch, the iPhone app copies your bikes' settings to the watch, and rides you time on the watch come back to the iPhone's service log. This goes directly between your iPhone and your watch through Apple's watch connection; we never receive it.

Apple Health and Fitness

If you allow it, the watch app records each timed ride as an Outdoor Cycle workout in Apple Health, so it shows in the Fitness app and counts toward your Activity rings. During the ride it reads your heart rate and calories to show them on the timer.

If you also allow the iPhone app to read workouts, it reads your cycling workouts (when each started and ended, and which app saved it), so rides you record with other apps, such as Garmin Connect, Strava or Trailforks, count toward your fork and shock service hours. It reads them on your iPhone and keeps only the rides you add to the service log.

Plushbucket never sends Health data to us or anyone else and never uses it for advertising. You can turn access off any time in the Health settings on your iPhone.

Health Connect (Android)

If you allow it, the Android app reads the bike rides other apps saved to Health Connect (when each started and ended, and which app saved it), so rides you record with apps such as Garmin Connect or Strava count toward your service hours. It only reads rides and never writes to Health Connect. The data stays on your phone, we never receive it, and it's never used for advertising. You can turn access off any time in Health Connect.

Backup files

A backup file is made only when you tap Save backup file. It goes wherever you choose to save or send it, and we never see it.

Services involved in delivering the app

  • Google Fonts: when you're online, the app and website download their typeface from Google, which receives your IP address as part of that request. See Google's privacy policy.
  • Netlify hosts plushbucket.com and keeps standard server logs, such as IP addresses and pages requested. See Netlify's privacy policy.
  • Apple and Google deliver the apps through the App Store, TestFlight and Google Play under their own privacy policies. If you join a beta and send feedback or crash reports, Apple or Google passes those to us.
  • Ko-fi: the website has an optional link to Ko-fi for tips. Nothing is sent to Ko-fi unless you tap it; anything you do there is covered by Ko-fi's privacy policy.

Children

Plushbucket is made for adult riders and isn't directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
