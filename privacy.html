Privacy policy

Last updated November 2, 2026. Covers the Plushbucket website (plushbucket.com) and the Plushbucket apps for iPhone, iPad and Android.

The short version

Plushbucket has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. What you enter stays on your device. We don't receive it, sell it or share it.

What stays on your device

Your bikes, forks and shocks, your weight, slider settings, service dates, ride hours and trail notes are saved only on your phone, tablet or computer (in the app, or in your browser's storage on the website). They're used only to calculate your settings and service reminders. Deleting the app or clearing your browser data erases them.

Backup files

A backup file is made only when you tap Save backup file. It goes wherever you choose to save or send it, and we never see it.

Services involved in delivering the app

  • Google Fonts: when you're online, the app and website download their typeface from Google, which receives your IP address as part of that request. See Google's privacy policy.
  • Netlify hosts plushbucket.com and keeps standard server logs, such as IP addresses and pages requested. See Netlify's privacy policy.
  • Apple and Google deliver the apps through the App Store, TestFlight and Google Play under their own privacy policies. If you join a beta and send feedback or crash reports, Apple or Google passes those to us.

Children

Plushbucket is made for adult riders and isn't directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
