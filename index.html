          What fits your shock

            Balance check: front and rear sag

            Measure sag at both ends, the same way each time, and the app tells you which end to change. A bike that sits level front to rear steers and grips the way it was designed to.

            Bracketing: find your best click

            Ride the same short section three times: where your dial is now, 2 clicks firmer and 2 clicks softer. Then pick the one that felt best. Feeling the difference teaches you a dial faster than reading about it.

              Shop mode: hand over a setup

              For mechanics. Record what you set on a customer's bike, then give them a link or QR code. When they open it, Plushbucket shows your settings and can add the bike to their garage.

                How to pump it up

                  Click numbers count from fully closed: turn the dial clockwise until it stops, then count clicks counterclockwise. Compression that starts "fully open" is turned counterclockwise until it stops.
